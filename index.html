BEFORE YOU GO

What's your purpose?

Make a conscious choice before opening.

If you do not see a green ClearFeed timer badge after opening Instagram, the filter is NOT active. Use Copy link, open Safari, and paste it in the address bar. Opening inside a Home Screen app or in-app browser may bypass Safari extensions.
HOW IT WORKS

Two parts, one purpose.

1. This Home Screen app gives you intentional launch shortcuts and stores your chosen daily budgets locally on this device.

2. The Safari Userscripts filter hides Instagram Reels/Explore and X For You, and enforces a visible-use daily timer on each website. ClearFeed passes your chosen daily budget when launching a site.

Important: You need to open the social sites in Safari with Userscripts enabled. iOS Home Screen web apps do not themselves filter outside websites. To reliably use the filter, copy a social-site link from the launch dialog and paste it into the regular Safari app. A green ClearFeed badge confirms the script loaded. If it is absent, Reels are NOT blocked. Changing the settings here doesn't override Apple's Screen Time.

Nothing is uploaded by ClearFeed. It does not ask for your passwords.
